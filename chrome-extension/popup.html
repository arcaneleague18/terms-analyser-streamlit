Setup Required

Chrome Built-in AI (Gemini Nano) is not enabled in your browser. Please follow these steps to enable it:

  1. Open chrome://flags/#prompt-api-for-gemini-nano and set to Enabled.
  2. Open chrome://flags/#optimization-guide-on-device-model and set to Enabled BypassPerfRequirement.
  3. Relaunch your browser.
  4. Go to chrome://components and click Check for update under Optimization Guide On-Device Model. Wait for the download to complete.
