Chrome Built-in AI (Gemini Nano) is not detected. Follow these steps to enable local inference:

  1. Navigate to chrome://flags/#prompt-api-for-gemini-nano and set to Enabled.
  2. Navigate to chrome://flags/#optimization-guide-on-device-model and set to Enabled BypassPerfRequirement.
  3. Relaunch Chrome.
  4. Open chrome://components and click Check for update under Optimization Guide On-Device Model.
